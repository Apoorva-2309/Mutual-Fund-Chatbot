# Understanding Groww Trading Charges

- **Brokerage:** Groww's published pricing lists equity brokerage as ₹20 or 0.1% of trade value, whichever is lower, subject to a minimum charge. F&O brokerage is listed separately.
- **Other trading costs:** Exchange transaction charges, statutory levies and applicable taxes can contribute to the total cost of a trade.
- **DP charges:** These generally apply when shares are debited from a demat account following a sale. The applicable amount and taxes depend on the published pricing terms.
- **Cancelled orders:** A cancelled order and a completed trade are different situations. Groww's help centre explains when blocked order funds are released; do not assume a reported deduction is a fee without checking the transaction details.
- **How to verify charges:** Review the transaction details and contract note, which provide a breakdown of the charges applied to a trade.
- **Customer guidance:** Before trading, check the current pricing schedule and estimate the total costs for your specific order. If a deduction is unclear, compare the transaction record with the contract note and contact official support.
